Invertis University is a leading institution for higher education and research, committed to helping students learn, build, and innovate.

About Invertis University

Located in Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh, Invertis University provides a welcoming and inspiring environment for students from diverse backgrounds. Our academic programs combine strong foundations with practical experience so that students are ready to meet the needs of a changing world.

Our dedicated faculty guide students through classroom learning, projects, research, and industry interaction. We encourage curiosity, creativity, teamwork, and ethical leadership in every student.

Campus Experience

Students enjoy a supportive campus community with opportunities to participate in clubs, cultural activities, sports, workshops, seminars, and career development programs. These experiences help students grow personally as well as professionally.
